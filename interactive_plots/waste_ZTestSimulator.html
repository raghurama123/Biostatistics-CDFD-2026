Interactive Z-Test Simulator

One-sample z-test for a population mean

Use this simulator when the population standard deviation $\sigma$ is known.

The null hypothesis is

$$ H_0:\mu=\mu_0 $$

and the z statistic is

$$ \boxed{ z= \frac{\bar X-\mu_0} {\sigma/\sqrt{n}} } $$
What should be entered? Enter the hypothesized mean $\mu_0$, the observed sample mean $\bar X$, the known population standard deviation $\sigma$, and the sample size $n$. If $X$ is a single observation rather than a sample mean, use $n=1$.

Interpretation of the three tests

Left-tailed: $H_1:\mu<\mu_0$. Small negative z values provide evidence against $H_0$.

Right-tailed: $H_1:\mu>\mu_0$. Large positive z values provide evidence against $H_0$.

Two-tailed: $H_1:\mu\ne\mu_0$. Large positive or negative z values provide evidence against $H_0$.
